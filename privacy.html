Privacy Policy

App: yt_upload · Last updated: November 16, 2026

Who uses this app

yt_upload is a private tool used only by the owner of the UnD YouTube channel to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload videos to the owner's channel;
  • post and read comments on the owner's own videos;
  • read the channel's own statistics and analytics (views, watch time, audience country).

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person.
  • It does not access any channel or account other than the owner's own.
  • Analytics are aggregated reports provided by YouTube; no individual viewer is identified.

Storage and deletion

The OAuth tokens are stored privately and are used only for the purposes above. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions can be left as a comment on the UnD channel.
